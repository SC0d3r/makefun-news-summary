World leaders took the stage at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, with host Turkey pressing for a new climate deal; AFP reported that several major leaders were absent. (Reuters, AFP)

In Abu Dhabi, US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine continued into a second day. President Volodymyr Zelensky said he was "happy" with a change in the US approach, while US envoy Jared Kushner said Washington had "narrowed the gaps"; Kyiv insists on security guarantees. (AFP)

Millions of Spanish workers joined the country's first general strike in 14 years, demanding action on a housing crisis less than three weeks before a national election that could bring the far right to power. Average rents have almost doubled in a decade. (AP)

Global bonds tumbled and the dollar held firm after a hot US inflation print fuelled fears of a jumbo Federal Reserve rate hike. Gold edged higher after a near-3% slide, and oil rebounded as attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz blunted hopes of an Iran truce. (Reuters)

The US midterms put the pro-Israel lobby AIPAC on the defensive, with Senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed mockingly thanking it and James Talarico saying he had sworn off its funding. The scandal surrounding Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang revived a push in Washington for a tougher line on China. (AFP)

A public bus crashed into several vehicles in Bundang, South Korea, leaving nine people seriously injured and 48 hurt. (Reuters)

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo passed 11,000 cases with 5,387 deaths, Al Jazeera reported.
